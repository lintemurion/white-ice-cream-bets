# How to use White Ice Cream Bets

The group's place to make bets, decide who won, and keep track of who owes what.

**Site:** https://whiteicecreambets.web.app

## Getting in

1. Open the site and sign in with Google.
2. Pick the name the group will see.
3. Wait to be voted in. Once more than half the group says yes, you're in.

**Tip:** on iPhone, open the site in Safari and use Share > **Add to Home Screen** so it opens like an app. On Android, use the browser menu > **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Making a bet

1. Tap **+ New Bet**.
2. **What's the bet?** For example, "Jo passes the driving test before March."
3. **About someone?** If the bet is about someone who controls the outcome, pick them. That person can't be one of the two sides, so nobody can win a bet they can just make happen.
4. **Me vs:** pick who you're betting against.
5. **If I lose / If they lose:** what each side owes. It can be anything: "$5", "a ride to the mall", "a friendship bracelet". The two can be different.
6. **Votes:** choose **Open** or **Anonymous** (see below).
7. Tap **Propose bet**. The other person has to **Accept** before it counts. They can also **Decline**, and you can **Cancel** until they accept.

## Deciding who won

1. When it's time, anyone taps **Call it**. Voting opens.
2. Everyone votes for who won, including the two people betting and the person it's about. You can change your vote until it's decided. Tapping your current vote again takes it back.
3. As soon as one side has votes from more than half the group, the bet is settled.

**Open votes:** everyone can see who voted which way, and the count shows as votes come in.

**Anonymous votes:** everyone can see who has voted, but not which way. The count stays hidden until the bet is decided or everyone has voted.

Votes on new people joining are always anonymous.

## Paying up

A settled bet shows who owes whom and what. When the loser pays up, the **winner** taps **Mark paid**.

## Good to know

- Your name: tap **Profile** to change it.
- Only the group can see anything. People who aren't in the group just see the sign-in screen.
- Kyle runs the site and can fix mistakes, like a bet with a typo or a vote that needs redoing. Kyle doesn't vote and doesn't count toward any majority.
- "Anonymous" means the app never shows how anyone voted. Someone very determined, using browser developer tools, could work it out, and Kyle can see everything in the Firebase console. Nobody can change who wins, though.
